# Long Haul: lived-in cockpit briefs for the five ships

A redesign of the five cockpits under the same rules as [the deck briefs](../ship-interiors/briefs.md): tactile controls only, fifteen years of hard use, every surface doing a job. The [first cockpit briefs](briefs.md) describe the models that are in the game today. This page describes what they should become.

## What every cockpit keeps

The game hangs its fittings on fixed anchors, so these stay where they are on all five hulls.

| Fitting | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Six live instrument faces | main, left, right, pedestal, overhead and radio |
| Flight stick | right hand |
| Mug and clipboard | left hand |
| Mascot | on the dash, right of centre |
| Two wipers | on the windshield |
| Two cabin lamps | overhead, behind the pilot |
| Sliding door | centre of the back wall, into the corridor |

- **Size.** One seat. About 1.8 m wide and 2.1 m from windshield to door. The ceiling is about 1.9 m and lower at the overhead panel.
- **Window shapes and colours** stay as they are per ship, so each hull is still recognised from the shape of the sky.
- **The six live faces** each sit in a deep hooded bezel, so they read as recessed amber or green tubes. Around each bezel go toggle switches, lit push-buttons and dial gauges. No flat glass panels.
- **No glowing edge strips.** Glow comes from gauge faces, button caps and shaded lamps.
- **Readable from the seat.** Clutter is chunky. Any lettering is at least 0.25 m tall on the walls and bold on the dash.

## 1. Rustbucket Mk I

*A wheelhouse welded between two drum saddles.*

- **Layout.** A broad, uneven trapezoid windshield with a triangular repair clamp at the upper left. A round inspection window to port and a short pane to starboard, squeezed between exhaust pipes. The roof steps up toward the smokestack. A patchwork steel slider at the back.
- **Hardware.** Three dark steel instrument pans on open brackets, with daylight between them. A throttle quadrant of two iron levers with the paint worn to bare metal at the grips. A brass knife switch for the main bus.
- **Repairs.** (1) A copper pipe mended with an oversize collar. (2) One pan re-hung on a length of chain because its bracket sheared. (3) A missing service plate, the hole covered with a biscuit-tin lid and four odd screws.
- **Light.** Two amber cabin lamps, one in an enamel shade that belongs to a different lamp. Amber tube glow on the pans. Soot under the roof vent.
- **Clutter.** A sprung brown seat with one corduroy cushion tied down. A tin mug in a gimbal ring. A laminated start-up checklist with step six rewritten in pen. A paper star chart taped to the roof. A cardigan over the seat back.

## 2. Dromedary 400

*The raised box over the container deck.*

- **Layout.** One broad rectangular windshield with clipped upper corners. Square side windows set deep in stacked cargo frames. Two square roof rails run back to the door. Replaceable tread panels underfoot.
- **Hardware.** Squared instrument cases hooked on a continuous rail, each with its own row of breakers. A load-balance panel of fourteen lit push-buttons, one for each hold unit. A dead-man lever on the pedestal.
- **Repairs.** (1) One instrument case replaced with a model from a different year, a finger's width too narrow, shimmed with folded card. (2) A cracked side window braced with a yellow cargo strap. (3) A breaker taped in the ON position, with "LEAVE" written on the tape.
- **Light.** Soft amber from the clerestory, falling on dark panels. Yellow button caps. A clip lamp on the bill-of-lading pocket.
- **Clutter.** A charcoal seat with a flat folding arm. A tie-down buckle holding exactly one pencil. A wheel chock in its labelled cubby. A stack of manifests under a wrench. A sun visor made from a clipboard.

## 3. Stellar Stoat

*A teardrop pressure shell that follows the swept hull.*

- **Layout.** One gently curved windshield meeting two long cheek panes. A single slender roof bow behind the pilot's brow. Side consoles sweep forward and end in level pads for the stick and mug. A ribbed rubber heel trough runs back to a flush pocket door.
- **Hardware.** A dinner-plate tachometer at the centre of the main face. A short gated throttle with a red lock-out collar. Toggle rows under sprung guards along both consoles.
- **Repairs.** (1) Two switch guards missing, the bare toggles marked with racing tape. (2) The pocket door runs on one roller and a wedge. (3) A crazed cheek pane patched from inside with a clear disc and a ring of sealant.
- **Light.** Two small shaded dome lamps and teal dial faces. This replaces the turquoise edge lamps, so the outside view stays dark.
- **Clutter.** A low two-shell bucket seat with a teal belt on a spool. A spare visor in a sleeve cut to shape. A stopwatch hung from the roof bow. Lap times in grease pencil on the left console. One driving glove.

## 4. Opulent Manatee

*The forward lounge of the liner, wrapped round the pilot.*

- **Layout.** A gently bowed windshield flanked by a tall pane each side, with a shallow roof light above. Gold window bands continue across the side returns. Low walnut cabinets carry the instruments like writing desks. Thick carpet up to a hard threshold at the leather-inset door.
- **Hardware.** Brass-rimmed round gauges set into walnut. Levers with turned wooden grips, worn pale. A brass speaking tube to the corridor with a cork on a chain.
- **Repairs.** (1) A walnut panel replaced in plywood, stained the wrong brown. (2) A lost gauge bezel replaced with a curtain ring. (3) The seat heater switch replaced with a household light switch.
- **Light.** Fringed amber lamps grazing the wood from under the upper trim. Warm dial faces. Satin wood and leather, so stars stay visible in the glass.
- **Clutter.** A heated oxblood club chair with a scalloped back and a darned arm. A brass mug cradle with a porcelain liner. A felt blotter for the clipboard. Fluted warmers holding folded towels. A footstool.

## 5. Quasar Empress

*A pearl arch that splits into three buttresses behind the pilot.*

- **Layout.** A wide unbroken panorama ahead, with tapered side lights wrapping back past the shoulders. Dark instrument islands rise on slim pearl supports. A raised floor with inlaid arcs and a clear centre aisle to the door.
- **Hardware.** A horseshoe of lit push-buttons round the main face. A two-handed warp lever in a pearl gate. A ring-brake handwheel on the pedestal.
- **Repairs.** (1) One instrument island's pearl support replaced with a length of scaffold tube. (2) Half the push-buttons dark, the working ones marked with nail varnish. (3) A grey primer plate where a pearl panel was, with "PAINT ME" written on it.
- **Light.** Shaded lamps set into the arch, behind frosted lenses, and cool blue dial faces. This replaces the concealed cyan sources and the light that travelled along the trim.
- **Clutter.** An ivory throne seat with a cushion that does not match. A cup socket in a small ring, holding a chipped mug. A plinth long enough for one mascot. A list taped to the dash titled "THINGS TO FIX WHEN RICH". A blanket.

## The audit

- **No wasted space.** Side returns carry lockers, the roof carries the overhead face and a chart, and the floor is clear only where feet and the door need it.
- **Maintenance access.** Every instrument case comes off its bracket or rail from the front. Pipe and cable runs are exposed along the sills.
- **Nothing factory-fresh.** Each cockpit has three visible repairs and wear at the grips, the heel rest and the door track.

## What this needs before it is in the game

These are briefs only. The five cockpit models have not been rebuilt. Rebuilding them means new Blender models on the same anchors, new renders, and a check that the six live faces, the lever sweeps and the windshield still fit.
