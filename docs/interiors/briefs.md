# Long Haul shop interiors

Thirty rooms at thirteen ports. The first three belong to Vendorhaven, where retired vending machines have found three different uses for their old equipment. Those rooms form the Blender pilot after design approval.

## From the shop camera

At Bureau Nine, the depot shelf fills much of the left side, the keeper’s speech bubble crosses the middle, and the cantina’s passenger cards can hide the counter front. Give each design a shape that survives those overlaps. Carry its outline across a ceiling edge or down a side return, and keep a quieter field immediately behind the moving crew. In a small grayscale view, the room, keeper and crew should still read as separate shapes. Model lettering at least 0.25 m high wherever a sign is needed.

Counter treatments below describe finishes on the fixed 4.4 × 0.8 m counter. Each keeps the top flat at 1.05 m and reserves a flat 3.0 × 0.8 m name-plate panel. In depots, the named rack is the playable goods shelf, with 4.2 × 0.9 m shelves at 1.0, 2.15 and 3.3 m. Dressings stay outside the twelve display slots. In every depot, keep the back-right door open, fit shelves down both sides of the stockroom and leave the straight 1.4 m lane to its packing bench. Recess side furnishings and suspend overhead pieces above the actors’ reach so the droid, crate run, engine stand, mug act and signpost retain their places. The full dimensions and export checks are in the [interior handoff](../handoff-bespoke-interiors.md).

Use the planet skies and terrain as palette anchors. Candara and Jovexa look out on cloud from suspended decks. The four research stations retain metal pressure decks, with stars and station hardware outside glazed openings. Pair visible emissive fixtures with two to five light markers and keep the game’s vertex-lighting budget in view. The [planet palettes](../../src/01-data.js) and [venue names, keepers and dialogue](../../src/08b-venues.js) are the game references.

## Verdanthia · Vendorhaven

### Dispatch Bay B4

**Potting shed with a freight clock.** A high row of sawtooth greenhouse panes steps down toward the stockroom. Leaf-green enamel ribs, cream ceramic troughs and a terracotta counter plinth belong to the retired vending machines that tend Vendorhaven. Train one pear tree flat against the back-left wall, its crown visible above the goods shelf. Beside the stock door, the dispatch unit has fitted a contract chute and a second outlet with a soup bowl underneath. Cool daylight picks out the leaves. An amber dial lights the last dispatch of the day.

*Cargo depot · Dispatch unit.* Terracotta potting-bench counter and a cream seed-tray goods rack with green uprights.

### Fix-O-Matic 3000

**Unfolded vending-machine service cabinet.** The workshop fits inside the open back of an old vending cabinet. Two thick blue service doors fold along the side walls, exposing cream enamel recesses and a broad copper cooling coil. A sprung orange mallet hangs in a fitted recess on one door. Beside it, the repair unit keeps a velvet pad for the apology that follows the blow. Twin ivory inspection bars light the engine bay. A strip of moss along the high vent is all the garden the repair unit allows inside.

*Garage · Repair unit.* Cream enamel counter on a folded blue kick plate, with one orange latch on each end.

### The Coin Slot

**Orchard listening pavilion.** A low fan of bent pearwood opens toward the garden. Its wide ribs meet at an off-centre brass spindle on the back wall. Plum felt lines the fan, with a quiet patch behind the mug jugglers. The former jukebox keeps one record in a nearly empty wall rack. A cutaway coin return winds past a side booth and drops its coins into a tip saucer. Honey light washes the wood ribs, and a rose lamp over the single record marks the evening’s entire programme.

*Cantina · Bartender, former jukebox.* Pearwood counter with brass end rings and a plum felt inset behind the name plate.

## Scorchmaw · Port Gneiss

### Gravel Freight Yard

**Basalt quarry under a weighbridge.** Two stepped faces of nearly black basalt hold up a rust-red weighbridge. Its blunt underside makes a lintel above the stock door. A great white balance pointer left of the opening is visible above the goods, with a one-crate weighing pan parked in a side recess. The yard boss keeps a salt lick there for palate breaks between crates. A high slit in the rock shows the orange lava outside. Pale work lamps keep the crew legible against the soot.

*Cargo depot · Yard boss.* Sawn basalt counter and weighing-rack shelves carried on paired oxide-red brackets.

### Lava Lube & Patch

**Ceramic heat shield curled around a forge.** Overlapping ivory refractory tiles curl up the back wall into a hood, edged in oxblood iron. One cutaway reveals an orange lava feed behind the tiles. A copper patch press occupies the left wall, and two enormous oven mitts hang thumb-to-thumb from its release lever, ready for the fluffy welder. The opposite cheek of the hood carries a broad cool task light. It illuminates the engine and keeps the welder’s pale fur distinct from the hot seam.

*Garage · Very fluffy welder.* Glazed tile counter with a thick oxblood edge and a mitt-sized handle at either end.

## Dunemoor · Bureau Nine

### Form 27-B Cargo Office

**A civic archive cut into canyon strata.** Bands of ochre sandstone run straight into projecting paper-file dividers. Together they make a stepped archive wall with the stock door filed under one tab. Above the goods shelf, a cream ticket roll unspools toward a large two-number serving board. At the side, the Senior Clerk of Crates has pressed the official seal into a sandwich and filed it under lunch. Deep window reveals admit narrow shafts of desert light. Blue-black edges make the cream paper and counter easy to pick out.

*Cargo depot · Senior Clerk of Crates.* Sandstone counter with a cream writing surface and steel goods shelves on file-tab uprights.

### The Rubber Stamp

**Three ink-pad booths in a desert cellar.** A cloverleaf of low adobe vaults makes three drinking booths along the perimeter. Each has a burgundy seat recessed into a black rim like an ink pad. The bartender’s wall tap has one enormous stamp handle, three spouts and a single tray fitted to three glasses. Thick pale arches frame the darker jugglers’ backdrop. Small high windows catch the last sand-coloured daylight, while one warm opal lamp in each vault lights all three copies of the order.

*Cantina · Bartender.* Black rubber-edged counter with a burgundy inset and three ivory end-grain plugs.

## Sloshwell · Ladle Harbor

### Ladle Harbor Freight

**A tide lock with a floating freight gauge.** Broad blue-green lock gates, cream cork bumpers and a flat deck give the harbormaster a dry place to receive cargo on an ocean of broth. A float in a tall side window lifts a giant ladle-shaped pointer along a scale of bowl silhouettes. A capstan reel sits in a wall pocket, and a tide line stops halfway up the stock-door jambs. Cool blue light comes off the water. A butter-yellow harbour lamp makes the dry passage to the packing bench unmistakable.

*Cargo depot · Harbormaster.* Blue lock-beam counter with cork ends and cream goods shelves on blue gate brackets.

### The Bottomless Bowl

**A tasting room inside a double-walled tureen.** Ivory ceramic walls sweep up from the flat floor to a cobalt lip. A deep handle on each side frames a sea window. A round inspection glass in the left wall exposes three successive bowl rims, the smallest disappearing into dark glaze. The soup sommelier keeps three deep tasting ladles in a fan on the opposite wall. Blue harbour light catches the handles, and a warm stockpot lamp glazes the back of the counter with the colour of clear broth.

*Cantina · Soup sommelier.* White ceramic counter with a cobalt rim and an ochre foot ring around its base.

## Amethyne · Bargain Roost

### Bargain Roost Cargo Perch

**A cantilevered crystal auction balcony.** A violet crystal shelf juts from the back-left wall over a cutout view of the teal sea. The perch master auctions freight from a brass roost rail clamped to the crystal. An outsized two-pan bidding scale hangs above the goods shelf. One pan holds a price tag, which has acquired a smaller tag of its own. Pale pink daylight catches the bevels against dark plum rock. Warm light under the cantilever leads across to the stock door.

*Cargo depot · Perch master.* Smoky violet counter ends and a brass goods rack with perch-shaped outer brackets.

## Toxibloom · Mellow Hollow

### Mellow Hollow Depot

**A freight basket caught between mangrove roots.** Three great roots grip the outside of a woven reed storehouse. The belly of the basket shows through wide gaps between them. Inside, olive wicker bands wrap the counter base and goods rack. A cork cargo float hangs high and dry from the back-left root. A crate embedded in that root has been waiting long enough for the wood to grow around it. The unhurried keeper works by cream marsh daylight and a row of pear-green fungus cups along the stockroom lintel.

*Cargo depot · Depot keeper.* Woven counter and reed-lashed shelf uprights with plain sand-coloured goods trays.

### Spore & Bolt

**A workshop under one bracket fungus.** A single shelf fungus grows out of the left wall and spans the bay like a cantilever roof. Thick cream gills fan toward its free edge above the engine. Plum-black bog iron and green glazed clay form the wall benches. The mechanic holds patches in a slow screw press until a fruiting cap emerges from its collar. A second, fully grown collar hangs on the wall as the reference. Amber gill tips and two pale task lamps light the work.

*Garage · Mechanic.* Plum iron counter with a clay top and two thick gill brackets tucked under its ends.

### The Damp Log

**A taproom in the split seam of a fallen trunk.** The room follows the length of a hollow trunk. One long split opens onto the swamp, while a cut end at the back shows thick rings of dark umber heartwood and ochre sapwood. The bartender taps two bulbous growths still attached to the side wall. One feeds a ceramic jug, the other wears a loosely tied privacy curtain. A warm amber seam runs along the split. Mint light from the taps silhouettes the jugs away from the mug act.

*Cantina · Bartender.* Split-log counter with a planed honey-coloured top and bark retained on each end.

## Candara · Port Lament

### Port Lament Cargo Dock

**A flying dock under a slack sail.** Two masts outside the platform hold a dusty-rose freight sail whose centre hangs in a deep curve above the crew. Pink clouds fill the tall openings on either side of the enclosed stockroom. The dockmaster has rigged a windsock shaped like a bellows and a violet wall drum carrying one extravagantly long mooring line. Neither working end reaches the floor. Pearl daylight outlines the droop. A warm lantern under the stock lintel gives arriving cargo a more cheerful reception than its keeper does.

*Cargo depot · Dockmaster.* Violet mooring-cleat counter ends and a cream goods rack tensioned at its outer corners.

### The Sunk Cost

**The listing saloon of a failed cloud ferry.** A retired ferry cabin hangs level on its floating platform, but its oxblood ribs and oval windows lean to port. A level brass counter rail and the horizontal pink cloud horizon make the list apparent. A plumb bob hangs inside the left window. The bartender keeps a large reversible closing paddle beside it, with the same closing bell embossed on both faces. Pink evening light rakes the ribs. A solitary amber reading globe stays lit over the empty last booth.

*Cantina · Bartender.* Oxblood cabin counter with a horizontal brass foot rail ending short of the aisle.

## Jovexa · The Drift

### Drift Cargo Bubble

**An inflated freight envelope tied to a rigid deck.** Wide cream air cells curve over the cargo floater’s head, cinched by three thick burnt-orange webbing belts. The stockroom is a rigid pressure box stitched into the back. Golden cloud banks show through kidney-shaped windows. A tethered mail float waits in a high wall cradle, its chunky return spool mounted by the door for contracts that drift away. Soft almond daylight fills the envelope. Blue-white valve lamps mark the shelf ends against the warmer skin.

*Cargo depot · Cargo floater.* Apricot counter with ivory seam caps and a goods rack bound at its ends by orange belts.

### Gentle Repairs

**A hull cradle suspended in a cloud veranda.** Two padded crescent jaws curve inward from the side walls above a flat suspended deck, leaving the engine bay open between them. Apricot canvas, deep petrol-blue steel and cream rubber give each jaw a distinct edge against the gold clouds. The hull whisperer’s oversized listening cup hangs from a coiled hose. Beside it, a practice dent sits between two cushion pads with the faintest dimple left. Soft amber side light and a milky inspection globe keep the workshop restful.

*Garage · Hull whisperer.* Petrol-blue counter with cream bumper ends and a broad, apricot top.

## Brrrindle · Fort Snuggle

### Fort Snuggle Cargo Hut

**A scarf-bound emergency A-frame.** Paired timber rafters make a steep triangle over walls of blue snow block. A thick vermilion scarf wraps the high joint and trails down the far side of the goods rack, clear of the goods. The cargo cuddler keeps a wall-mounted yarn reel and a crate-sized scarf gauge by the stock door. A notch window shows a blue drift banked against the hut. Cold light runs along the rafters while a gold packing lamp warms the timber counter.

*Cargo depot · Cargo cuddler.* Timber counter on snow-block feet and a mitten-red goods rack with timber trays.

### Warm Wrench Garage

**A thaw shed built around a radiator.** The back wall is a bank of thick, upright enamel radiator fins under a shallow industrial roof. Two frosted end windows expose the snow outside. The mechanic keeps a drawer of warm wrenches in one flank and stretches a red engine jumper over a drying frame on the other. A brown scorch hole reveals one bright fin through the knitting. Warm copper light gathers between the fins. Cool blue window light outlines the central engine against a quiet slate floor.

*Garage · Mechanic.* Slate counter with a copper top rim and two rounded radiator ends.

### The Warm Mitten

**A cocoa parlour tucked into a snowbank.** A low arched snow ceiling meets two deep red felt-lined bays. In the left bay, a framed relief of a fire has a visible hanging cord and plug. It is the cocoa keeper’s entire fireplace. A broad copper cocoa urn fills the other bay, with a cup hanging beneath each of its two taps for anyone who still looks cold. A wide frost window admits icy blue light, and a honey glow from the picture reaches the timber-faced bar.

*Cantina · Cocoa keeper.* Dark timber counter with felt side pockets and a pale cocoa-coloured top.

## Listening Post Hush · The Quiet Ear

### Hush Receiving Bay

**A loading lock hung inside a second hull.** A pale grey rectangular inner lock floats inside a dark pressure hull on four oversize violet rubber mounts. A side viewport frames a slice of the listening dish against stars. The receiving officer lowers freight onto felt pads from a wall hoist. Its bell has been packed in a fitted felt case, with the clapper sticking out. Two long opal lamps graze the gap between hulls. A warmer pool inside the stockroom gives the droid lane a clear destination.

*Cargo depot · Receiving officer.* Inner-lock grey counter and a goods rack with violet rubber pads under its uprights.

### Soft Landing Repairs

**An acoustic test chamber with a wall of dent casts.** Rows of deep, blunt indigo acoustic wedges cover one sloping wall inside the metal hull. The other wall is smooth and holds three large plaster casts, each preserving a different dent the surveyor has repaired and grown fond of. A padded two-legged contour gauge hangs in the empty fourth space. Cold white strips rake the casts from the side so their profiles show. A warm inspection lamp picks out the live engine on the flat, uninterrupted deck.

*Garage · Dent surveyor.* Smooth indigo counter with chalk-white ends cast from the same mould as the dent samples.

### The Indoor Voice

**A quilted whispering gallery.** A low oval of overlapping felt petals lines the pressure cabin. The petals open at one end onto a narrow star window, with the dish rim just visible. Two facing booths sit inside the side walls. The bartender keeps padded tumblers in a deep oxblood rack and collects clapping fines in a velvet-lined till shaped like two palms. Warm lamps concealed inside the petals outline their scallops. Matt midnight blue behind the jugglers gives the flying mugs a clear silhouette.

*Cantina · Bartender.* Oxblood felt counter with a dark, smooth drinking surface and padded end rolls.

## Cryo Vault Rimeholm · The Freezer Aisle

### Cold Storage Intake

**A rolling specimen archive in a freezer lock.** Tall banks of cream insulated drawers flank a cyan pressure door. Their rounded shoulders and recessed black handles read like enormous index cards. A glazed specimen niche above the left rack holds one pancake upright in a blue ice disc. The intake curator has fitted the adjacent drawer with a broad paper reel for its life story. Cold white lamps wash the drawer faces in pairs. A single apricot cataloguing lamp marks the bench beyond the stock door.

*Cargo depot · Intake curator.* Insulated drawer-front counter and steel goods shelves with thick cream end caps.

### Thaw & Patch

**A defrost tunnel lined with split heater collars.** Three horseshoe heaters cross the ceiling in succession, from frost-blue ceramic at the rear to copper and ochre near the counter. A narrow side port gives a black star view through the pressure shell. The defrost mechanic keeps a vast bell-mouthed hairdryer cradled on one wall and a hinged warming pad, folded like two arms, on the other. Amber heater cores rim the hoops, while a clear white side lamp shows the engine’s dents beneath the warm light.

*Garage · Defrost mechanic.* Blue-grey counter with a copper edging strip that broadens toward its warm end.

## Calibration Spire · Tolerance Station

### Precision Freight

**A cargo hall inside a square measuring jig.** Three square steel frames nest against the back bulkhead, each edged in bone enamel. One corner of the outer frame exposes a brass adjustment screw as thick as a bollard. The tolerance clerk keeps an illuminated slot gauge beside the stock door and an accepted specimen hair stretched between two white jaws behind a magnifying lens the size of a crate. Neutral grazing light draws crisp edges across the charcoal deck. An amber work lamp singles out the hair for the benefit of visiting inspectors.

*Cargo depot · Tolerance clerk.* Graphite counter with bone-coloured corner blocks and a square-jointed brass goods rack.

### Exactly Right Repairs

**A torque laboratory built like a dial.** A broad ivory quadrant rises from a straight gunmetal base behind the engine. Thick cobalt divisions and a single orange pointer carry the scale around the back-left wall. The fitter checks every wrench against a suspended calibration weight in a recessed side bay. A spare bolt lives in a white fitted case with its own short pointer parked dead centre. Even white task light keeps the tick blocks distinct, with amber light reserved for the weight. A slit in the pressure hull shows stars.

*Garage · Fitter.* Gunmetal counter with ivory enamel edges and an orange end cap matching the pointer.

## Beacon Far Wink · The Last Light

### Last Light Freight Office

**A signal box astride a freight airlock.** A squat pressure cabin has a raised, glazed signal loft across the back-left half. Through the shutters are stars and the beacon’s service struts. Soot-grey steel, cream shutter blades and a red stock-door surround carry the station colours into the counter. The signal clerk works an outsized shutter key linked to a pair of receipt lamps, one short and one long. Their steady amber faces make a dot and dash above the shelf. A warm desk light spills through the loft glazing.

*Cargo depot · Signal clerk.* Cream counter with red shutter ends and a steel goods rack on signal-box brackets.

### End of the Line Garage

**A dead-end rail shed on the lamp service deck.** Two dark blue overhead rails come to a conspicuous stop against a butter-yellow buffer on the back bulkhead. A short, empty maintenance trolley stays parked high against one side. In a wall niche beneath it, the mechanic has given a retired engine cowling a folding seat and set a proper enamel teapot beside it. The working engine has the open central bay. Warm light under the buffer draws the eye to the end of the line, while a blue exterior work lamp outlines the hatch against the stars.

*Garage · Mechanic.* Blue steel counter with a yellow buffer at each end and a dark enamel worktop.

### The Final Blink

**The lighthouse lantern room after hours.** Broad bronze mullions divide a many-sided lantern room. Against the starfield, thick concentric lens segments form a luminous amber arc on the left, with a plain dark shutter behind the jugglers. The lamp-keeper pours from a wall-mounted oil-can tap and keeps a stout dimmer lever at the bright end of its long travel. One cream service lamp lights the bar independently of the lens. A dark sector and a lit sector suggest the beacon’s turn in a single still view.

*Cantina · Lamp-keeper and bartender.* Bronze counter with amber lens-glass end cheeks and a charcoal top.
