Point at your destination, set throttle, press Z. The drive drops out by itself near a planet. Fly on toward it and the port brings you down automatically. Deliveries pay on landing.

Side work: answer maydays (or don't), mine the asteroid fields on the map and sell ore at any port, take risky contraband runs, or escort nervous VIPs. Your reputation changes what people pay you and how hard customs look at you.
